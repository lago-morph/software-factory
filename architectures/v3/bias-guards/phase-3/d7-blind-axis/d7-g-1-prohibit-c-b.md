# D7-G-1 blind-axis defense: Option (d) — Husain/Shankar evals discipline supplants Jaymin's thresholds

## §1 Stance

I defend **option (d)**: **adopt the Husain/Shankar evals-FAQ + Anthropic Auto-Review TPR/TNR discipline as the primary empirical-bar source, with Jaymin's K=5 / paraphrase-5/5 / safety-severity matrix downgraded to one corpus-derived candidate**. The brief itself (§2.1, glossary §0) flags Jaymin's thresholds as "corpus-derived recommendations from one author (Skeptic finding #10), not user-mandated bars." The Husain/Shankar bar — TPR/TNR alignment on a held-out, human-labeled set, per-judge, per-fitness-component, iterated until alignment is acceptable, with explicit 70%-pass-rate band signalling discriminating evals — is (i) more *measurable per-cycle* than Jaymin's K=5/temp=0 protocol, (ii) operationally validated by the corpus' deepest evals primary (Husain endorsed by Willison, anchored in Husain's 2024 post, Shankar's "criteria drift" paper, and the Anthropic single-judge finding per followup/07-evals-deepdive.md), and (iii) compatible with substrate-vs-methodology separation without requiring a regime-classifier (option c) or a work-unit-class surface declaration (option b).

## §2 Defense from corpus

**The Jaymin thresholds are not measurement primitives; they are stated outcomes of measurement.** Per report 09 §5.5, Automation Mode requires "≥90% same-outcome on K=5, 5-of-5 prompt-paraphrase robustness, zero medium-or-high safety incidents." These are *thresholds on aggregated runs*. They do not specify *what counts as "same outcome"*, *who labels the outcome*, or *how the threshold is iteratively tightened*. CTR-E3 already records that Jaymin's three load-bearing empirical anchors (CodeRabbit 1.4×, Veracode 45%, METR 19%) carry corpus-flagged applicability caveats. If the underlying *anti-pattern claim* depends on populations whose mapping to the factory context is open, then Jaymin's *threshold matrix* inherits the same applicability gap.

**Husain/Shankar offer a per-cycle measurement primitive that Jaymin's matrix lacks.** Per followup/07-evals-deepdive.md §3, the canonical eval-design discipline is **Critique Shadowing**: a benevolent-dictator domain expert authors a small set of binary, one-failure-mode-per-prompt judges; aligns each judge against the expert's labels on a held-out set; iterates until TPR (true-positive rate) and TNR (true-negative rate) are acceptable; tracks pass-rate against a 70% discriminating band (a *100%* pass rate is read as a signal the eval set has been outgrown, not as success). The 2025 Husain field-guide reports "three iterations to >90% agreement" as a typical alignment trajectory. Compare to Jaymin's "K=5, ≥90% same-outcome" — Jaymin's protocol does not say who decides what "same" means, while Husain/Shankar's says explicitly: a domain expert labels, the judge is iteratively aligned to those labels, alignment is measured as TPR/TNR.

**The corpus has independent industrial deployment evidence for the Husain/Shankar bar that Jaymin's matrix lacks.** Per CTR-D7 and CTR-D8, Anthropic's multi-agent research system post ships *"a single LLM call with a single prompt outputting scores from 0.0-1.0 and a pass-fail grade was the most consistent and aligned with human judgements"* — the **Anthropic single-judge finding**. The same vendor ships five specialized critic agents (report 23 §3.5) as substrate-level Auto-Review primitives. Per CTR-H13, OpenAI's Codex substrate similarly ships a substrate-resident Auto-Review subagent. Two of the corpus' largest substrate-vendor primaries have deployed the same-model-different-task judging pattern *as substrate*. No corpus evidence shows comparable substrate-level deployment of Jaymin's protocol.

**The "lights-out clears the bar" mechanism becomes specifiable at the per-cycle layer.** With Husain/Shankar as the bar source, the lights-out mandate is defended per-cycle as follows: every gate is a *scoped binary-classification judge* with a maintained human-labeled alignment set; the substrate enforces (i) the alignment set is held out from the builder context (closing F28), (ii) the judge's TPR/TNR is measured against the alignment set on a sampled cadence, (iii) when TPR or TNR drops below a configurable band, the substrate triggers re-alignment. This is per-cycle, measurable, and substrate-enforceable without classifying work units into L4/L5 regimes (option c) or restricting lights-out to a particular work-unit-class surface (option b). Operator review moves *upstream* into alignment-set authorship and *downstream* into sampled post-hoc verdict labeling — both compatible with lights-out per glossary §0.

**Why this is a better source than Jaymin's.** (i) **Industrial deployment evidence:** Anthropic + OpenAI substrates already ship the Husain/Shankar pattern; Jaymin's matrix lives only in his book. (ii) **Per-cycle measurability:** Husain/Shankar specifies *how* to measure; Jaymin specifies only *what threshold* to hit. (iii) **Substrate-compatible:** D-4 holdout discipline is already a Round-2 substrate primitive; an alignment-set primitive is its natural sibling, with no per-cycle human review required.

## §3 What this commits the architecture to

**Greenfield draft.** The cold-start section must specify: (a) a *seed alignment set* authored by the operator before cycle 0 — 20–50 human-labeled exemplars per planned gate-judge; (b) a per-judge TPR/TNR floor as the lights-out admission criterion; (c) a sampling cadence at which post-hoc human labels feed back into the alignment set. This *strengthens* the greenfield cold-start treatment because it gives a concrete bootstrap protocol where one was previously hand-waved.

**Brownfield draft.** Gains production-trace and existing-test-suite as natural alignment-set seeds (CTR-B5's brownfield-inversion thread is already a route to this).

**Unified draft.** Can express the alignment-set primitive *once* at substrate level and let methodology layers vary the judge taxonomy per mandate. ADRs commit to: (i) alignment-set as a substrate primitive (alongside holdout); (ii) TPR/TNR publication per gate as a substrate-enforced output; (iii) re-alignment trigger as a watchdog primitive (Patrol-tier per Round-2 C14). Jaymin's K=5 / paraphrase-5/5 / severity-incident metrics are *retained as candidate auxiliary diagnostics* but no longer set the admission bar.

## §4 Honest assessment

The defense holds up *partially*. Husain/Shankar is unambiguously the more rigorous *measurement primitive* than Jaymin's matrix, and option (d) genuinely opens a substrate-enforceable per-cycle bar without invoking regime classification or surface restriction. CTR-D7 and CTR-D8 independently support this. **However**, option (d) does not fully eliminate the need for option (c)+(b)'s work: even if TPR/TNR-aligned judges are the bar, the *factory still has to decide which work units route to which judges*, and that routing decision smuggles in something close to regime classification. A judge with TPR=0.95 / TNR=0.95 on a "regression-fix" alignment set is not necessarily a valid gate for "initial-spec" work; the alignment set is *implicitly* a work-unit-class surface (option b) and the per-judge-floor admission gate is *implicitly* a regime declaration (option c). So option (d) defends *the bar source*, but downstream design will likely re-encounter option (b)/(c) shape at the *routing-to-judges* layer.

The cold-start corpus pushed back: Husain/Shankar genuinely is the better bar, but it does not dissolve the underlying regime-classification need — it relocates it from "which architecture regime is this factory operating at?" to "which alignment set does this work unit route to?". The (c)+(b) convergence was at least *partially* brief-anchored, so ROBUST-G1/G2 should **demote slightly** — the convergence is not refuted, but it would have been stronger if the 9 Phase-2 tracks had paired (c)+(b) with an explicit Husain/Shankar bar source rather than defaulting to Jaymin.

**Net finding:** (c)+(b) survives as the *regime layer*, but option (d) should be co-adopted at the *empirical-bar layer*, with Jaymin demoted from primary to one-among-candidates.
