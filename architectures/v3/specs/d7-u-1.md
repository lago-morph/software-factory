## §1 Overview

**Mandate.** Unified-attempt. D7-U-1 carries both greenfield and brownfield mandates parameterised by the **initial-FC-catalog density and opposing-side identity** at deployment time (per [D7-U-1 §1 "How the same architecture serves both mandates"](../bias-guards/phase-3/d7-blind-axis/d7-u-1-prohibit-interval-escrow.md#1-architecture-sketch)) rather than by architecture shape. The substrate is mandate-symmetric without using mandate as an axis-parameter.

**Axis.** Adversarial-falsification topology — every load-bearing artifact (spec / plan / code-change / eval / ADR / skill / classifier-decision / scaffold-edit) is parameterised by a typed **Falsification Commitment (FC)** declaring which opposing side (model-family-different agent / deterministic checker / named human / population vote) must try to falsify it before it can compound forward. The architecture is the **topology** of build/break pairs. The axis is unified-defensible because greenfield and brownfield differ only in the **initial catalog** of opposing sides — not in any substrate primitive's contract.

**Entry-mode.** Either greenfield (sparse initial FC catalog; operator-as-opposing-side at day-0; day-0 FC defaults are strictest per [D7-U-1 §5 "Cold-start"](../bias-guards/phase-3/d7-blind-axis/d7-u-1-prohibit-interval-escrow.md#5-cold-start)) or brownfield (rich initial FC catalog drawn from existing test suites, production telemetry, type system, dependency graph, and live users — each *already* serves as an opposing side via wrapper-FC re-classification per [d7-u-1.md §4](../substrate-requirements/d7-u-1.md)). X_UNM_B brownfield acquisition specified in §2.

**Methodology summary.** Substrate-heavy, thin-methodology. Per-cycle process is generic — agent creates an artifact, declares an FC against it, the opposing-side router resolves the handler, the refutation attempt runs, the verdict registers, the survival-window timer arms, and the compounding gate refuses downstream propagation unless the FC verdict is `survived` or `conditionally-survived-with-window` (inside the window). The Popperian conjecture-and-refutation loop is the default per-cycle methodology; FC-graph fanout is the variability surface.

**Load-bearing claim.** **Falsification commitment** — every work unit declares its falsification surface *before* execution; opposing-side judging is enforced by P-33 (ADR 0060); independence is *measured* by P-34 (ADR 0061), not declared; survival is timer-driven on P-30 (per [ADR 0064](../../../docs/adr/0064-p-30-variant-d7-survival-window.md)). The substrate refuses to compound artifacts without a survived FC — F53 (voluntary-discipline fragility) is closed by construction, not by operator discipline. The F1/F27/F46/F48 correlated-error cascade is the canonical four-mode mechanism that the FC primitive directly addresses at the substrate layer.

## §2 Substrate composition

D7-U-1 carries 8 substrate-layer ADRs beyond the common substrate baseline (0010-0017) and discipline ADRs (0018-0027): **3 framework + per-variant pairs (load-bearing)** + **2 D7-U-1-distinctive orphan ADRs**. The pairs and orphans are listed below in the order they appear in §0; each pair is named with the framework-ADR and the D7-U-1 per-variant ADR co-cited per [AGENTS-MD-a9fb7b42f8](../../../AGENTS.md#framework-adr-scope-boundary-discipline).

### Framework substrate with per-variant pairing

**P-28 FC envelope.** Framework: [ADR 0029](../../../docs/adr/0029-p-28-typed-object-store.md) records the substrate decision (libgit2 `git_odb_write` + `refs/notes/<envelope-kind>` namespaces, or Postgres `bytea`+`jsonb`+GIN alternate path; content-addressed append-only with JSON-Schema-at-`put`-time validation) shared across four contested variants. D7-U-1 variant: [ADR 0062](../../../docs/adr/0062-p-28-variant-d7-fc-envelope.md) registers the `falsification-commitment` envelope-kind. The [`overlap.md` verdict on P-28](../primitives/overlap.md#p-28-typed-object-store--four-contested-variants) is verbatim:

> **"Verdict: SAME primitive (P-28 typed-object store framework), DISTINCT envelopes. All four share the construction recipe (libgit2 `git_odb_write` + `refs/notes/<envelope-kind>`; Postgres `bytea`+`jsonb`+GIN alternate path; content-addressed append-only with typed envelope per P-28 sketch). The envelope schemas are non-overlapping but the underlying primitive (typed envelope + content-hash + append-only + Glean/Postgres-compatible storage) is identical."**

The D7-U-1 envelope is `FC{id, artifact, artifact-kind, conjecture, opposing-side.{kind, identity, independence-evidence}, refutation-attempt.{budget, method, inputs[]}, verdict.{outcome ∈ {survived, conditionally-survived-with-window, refuted}, counter-evidence[], survival-window}, ledger.{immutable-log-ref, trajectory-ref}}` with typed-filter axis `artifact-kind × verdict.outcome`. The envelope is **commitment-indexed rather than artifact-indexed** — it describes a commitment *about* an artifact, not the artifact itself — and is D7-U-1's load-bearing substrate handle. Per [u-1.md § P-28 contract](../substrate-requirements/d7-u-1.md), `artifact-kind ∈ {spec, plan, code-change, eval, adr, skill, classifier-decision, scaffold-edit}` is a closed enum; new kinds register via a `kind: methodology-delta` FC against the schema itself, preserving the substrate's reflexivity.

**P-29 compounding gate.** Framework: [ADR 0030](../../../docs/adr/0030-p-29-policy-mediator.md) records the shared engine (OPA Rego primary; Cedar alternate; content-addressed bundles; binding `allow / reasons[] / obligations[] / audit_envelope` verdict). D7-U-1 variant: [ADR 0063](../../../docs/adr/0063-p-29-variant-d7-fc-survival.md) records the FC-survival policy DSL. The [`overlap.md` verdict on P-29](../primitives/overlap.md#p-29-policy-mediator--compounding-gate--three-contested-variants) is verbatim:

> **"Verdict: SAME primitive (P-29 policy mediator framework), DISTINCT policy DSLs. All three share the underlying engine (OPA Rego primary; Cedar alternate path per P-29 sketch). The policy vocabulary differs: U-A reasons about interval-slot satisfaction; U-B reasons about layer-pair closure; D7-U-1 reasons about FC-survival windows. The differences are at the *predicate vocabulary* level, not the *engine* level."**

D7-U-1's DSL ([ADR 0063](../../../docs/adr/0063-p-29-variant-d7-fc-survival.md)) loads a Rego bundle in the `d7_u_1` namespace with `fc_survival(fc_id)` as the closure-condition vocabulary. The top-level rule `allow_compound[artifact_b]` walks the FC ledger (`data.fc_ledger`) and admits compounding iff every upstream FC about A has `verdict.outcome ∈ {survived, conditionally-survived-with-window}` AND (when conditionally) the cycle is inside `verdict.survival-window` (the policy uses `time.now_ns() <= expires_at_ns` so no separate expiry predicate is needed). This is the structural replacement for voluntary review (F53) and D-4 holdout discipline at every artifact boundary — the substrate refuses to compound un-survived artifacts.

**P-30 survival-window registrar.** Framework: [ADR 0036](../../../docs/adr/0036-p-30-event-registrar-substrate.md) records the shared Temporal substrate (signal+timer+query triad + append-only event-log + namespace separation). D7-U-1 variant: [ADR 0064](../../../docs/adr/0064-p-30-variant-d7-survival-window.md) records the survival-window state machine. The [`overlap.md` verdict on P-30](../primitives/overlap.md#p-30-event-registrar--two-contested-variants) is verbatim:

> **"Verdict: DISTINCT primitives despite shared underlying substrate. Both use Temporal workflow engine (signal+timer+query triad) at the construction layer, but the load-bearing semantics diverge: U-A's registrar is event-driven: state transitions on external triggers; the timer half is incidental (deadline tracking only). D7-U-1's registrar is timer-driven: the load-bearing transition is `survival-window-open → window-expired`, with cascade wake-up of dependent-FC graphs. The event half is incidental (verdict-rendered is the input event but the registrar's value-add is the post-verdict timer cascade)."**

The state machine is `FC-declared → opposing-side-running → verdict-rendered → survival-window-open → window-expired → re-falsification-required`. The durable `@timer` armed at `survival-window-open` entry fires when `verdict.survival-window` elapses; the handler reads the FC envelope's `ledger.immutable-log-ref`, enumerates dependent FCs (children whose `conjecture` depended on this FC's verdict), signals each with `@signal parent_window_expired(parent_fc_id)`, and writes a typed envelope to the append-only event log. Temporal's durable-timer guarantee (timers survive worker restarts and cluster failover) is the construction-layer property the cascade rests on. The named failure mode — *cascade-fail if the timer half is unreliable* — is mitigated but not eliminated; the compounding gate must treat absent-cascade as fail-closed (per [ADR 0064 § Harder](../../../docs/adr/0064-p-30-variant-d7-survival-window.md)).

### Orphan substrate (D7-U-1-distinctive)

**P-33 opposing-side router.** [ADR 0060](../../../docs/adr/0060-p-33-opposing-side-router.md) records D7-U-1's first orphan. The router consumes `FC.artifact-kind × FC.refutation-attempt.method × builder.family`, queries a capability registry mapping `(artifact-kind × method) → eligible handler classes`, and returns the resolved opposing-side handler — LLM-judge of distinct `(model-family, role)`, named deterministic checker (Python predicate), named human-of-record, or population-vote pool. Per the [Phase-4.2 P-33-vs-P-14 verdict](../primitives/overlap.md), P-33 is **DISTINCT** from P-14 despite sharing LiteLLM Router infrastructure: the load-bearing value-add is the **three-layer denylist** (call-time computed builder-family exclusion + LiteLLM tag-exclusion + registry family-allowlists) plus the broader handler universe (deterministic checkers, named humans, population votes are first-class registry-typed classes, not degenerate LLM-judge cases) plus the [ADR 0063](../../../docs/adr/0063-p-29-variant-d7-fc-survival.md) compounding-gate post-hoc check refusing any verdict where `Handler.family == artifact.builder.family`. The denylist is **unconditional** (not gated on a `cross-family` methodology flag) — Attractor's *do-not-unify* discipline (CTR-C4 in the corpus) is encoded as a substrate invariant, not a configurable policy.

**P-34 independence auditor.** [ADR 0061](../../../docs/adr/0061-p-34-independence-auditor.md) records D7-U-1's second orphan and its single **load-bearing RG primitive** (per [Phase-3.5.5 row](../candidate-registry.md#phase-355-rule-on-load-bearing-rg-primitives-binding-user-approved-2026-05-25)). The auditor is invoked by the P-29 compounding gate immediately before it writes `verdict.outcome` to the P-28 envelope; it runs a three-layer independence check against the FC's `refutation-attempt` envelope and the builder's [P-05 trajectory](../../../docs/adr/0012-p-05-trajectory-capture.md):

- **Model-family disjoint check** against the [P-24 attribution store](../../../docs/adr/0035-p-24-attribution-store.md) (D7-U-1 reads this via the shared sibling reference — P-24 is not a D7-U-1-owned primitive but is consumed read-only). Provider-only difference is insufficient.
- **Trajectory-overlap check** — opposing-side handler's prior-context window hashed against the builder's [P-05 trajectory (ADR 0012)](../../../docs/adr/0012-p-05-trajectory-capture.md); rolling-8-gram shingle-overlap threshold default `< 0.05`.
- **Population-vote subset check** — for `opposing-side.kind == population-vote`, no voter shares `model_family` with the builder; the pool's joint-distribution must cover all three of (family-disjoint, snapshot-distinct, training-window-distinct).

Any layer failing flips the verdict from `survived` → `inconclusive (auditor-flagged: <layer>)` and queues the FC for the **Option-C human-of-record reviewer** at audit cadence. The check is itself a deterministic scipy/sklearn-free function (set operations + rolling-hash + table lookup) — its **determinism is the Option-A assurance against recursion** (no LLM distribution shared with the judges it audits). Per [u-1.md §2 RG-primitives](../substrate-requirements/d7-u-1.md), the residual is the **A+C hybrid**: Option A (deterministic-ness IS the assurance) as primary plus Option C (named-human-of-record at audit cadence) as backstop reviewing the *distribution of findings* (not each FC). The structural recursion concern (who audits the auditor?) is the explicit **accept-as-RG** residual; F51 (Ashby-deficient probabilistic guard against novel collusion patterns) and the F42/F5 cognitive-ceiling surface re-introduced at the audit layer are accepted-open per [ADR 0061](../../../docs/adr/0061-p-34-independence-auditor.md).

### X_UNM_B brownfield acquisition

Per [u-1.md §4 X_UNM_B articulation](../substrate-requirements/d7-u-1.md), D7-U-1 addresses Codebase-Model acquisition from legacy artifacts as follows: for brownfield, D7-U-1 inherits a **rich initial FC catalog** from legacy artifacts — existing test suites, production telemetry, type systems, dependency graphs, and live users **already** serve as opposing sides. **No new extraction primitive is required when these artifacts are present**: existing tests + telemetry + type-checks *become* FC entries via wrapper FCs that re-classify their pass/fail signal as `verdict.outcome` against an inferred `conjecture`. The wrapper is a P-28 envelope-population step on top of legacy artifacts. **Completeness gap honestly named**: legacy systems without test coverage and without typed APIs cannot bootstrap an FC catalog from artifacts; these deployments degrade to greenfield-like operator-attested bootstrap. ## §3 Methodology shape

**Per-cycle loop (the falsification-commitment cycle).** Generic and thin. Each cycle:

1. **Artifact creation.** Agent (or operator) produces a typed artifact of `artifact-kind ∈ {spec, plan, code-change, eval, adr, skill, classifier-decision, scaffold-edit}`. The artifact is content-hashed and stored (in P-28 or in the codebase ODB for in-tree artifacts).

2. **FC declaration.** Agent declares one or more `FalsificationCommitment` envelopes against the artifact via [ADR 0062](../../../docs/adr/0062-p-28-variant-d7-fc-envelope.md). Each FC names `conjecture` (the falsifiable claim), `opposing-side.{kind, identity, independence-evidence}`, and `refutation-attempt.{budget, method, inputs[]}`. The envelope is `put` against the [ADR 0029](../../../docs/adr/0029-p-28-typed-object-store.md) substrate under `refs/notes/falsification-commitment`. JSON-Schema validation rejects malformed envelopes at `put` time.

3. **Opposing-side resolution.** The [P-33 opposing-side router (ADR 0060)](../../../docs/adr/0060-p-33-opposing-side-router.md) reads `FC.artifact-kind × FC.refutation-attempt.method` against the capability registry; for LLM-judge classes it computes `denylist := {artifact.builder.family}` at call-time and invokes `Router.get_available_deployment(model, request_kwargs={"tags": ["exclude:" + builder.family]})`; for deterministic / human / vote classes it returns the registered binary / identity / pool ID directly. The three-layer denylist makes builder-family exclusion **unconditional**.

4. **Refutation attempt.** The resolved handler runs the refutation under [P-02 cost ceilings (ADR 0011)](../../../docs/adr/0011-p-02-cost-ceilings.md) (sourced from `FC.refutation-attempt.budget`). Trajectory is captured by [P-05 (ADR 0012)](../../../docs/adr/0012-p-05-trajectory-capture.md). Watchdog tiers ([P-06, ADR 0013](../../../docs/adr/0013-p-06-watchdog-tiers.md)) escalate on budget-exhausted or anomalous-cost events.

5. **Independence audit (closure-time).** Before the P-29 compounding gate writes `verdict.outcome`, the [P-34 independence auditor (ADR 0061)](../../../docs/adr/0061-p-34-independence-auditor.md) runs the three-layer check (model-family disjoint, trajectory-overlap, population-vote subset). Pass → verdict registers as declared. Fail at any layer → verdict flips to `inconclusive (auditor-flagged: <layer>)` and the FC queues for Option-C human-of-record review at audit cadence.

6. **Verdict registration.** The verdict is written to the P-28 envelope. [P-30 survival-window registrar (ADR 0064)](../../../docs/adr/0064-p-30-variant-d7-survival-window.md) transitions the workflow `verdict-rendered → survival-window-open` and arms the durable timer.

7. **Compounding-gate enforcement.** Any boundary primitive about to expose A to downstream B serialises `{boundary: "compounding-gate", downstream_artifact, upstream_artifacts[]}` and calls the [ADR 0063](../../../docs/adr/0063-p-29-variant-d7-fc-survival.md) Rego bundle's `data.d7_u_1.allow_compound`. Result false → boundary refuses; `reasons[]` enumerates failing upstream FCs. Bundle hash is captured in `audit_envelope` per [ADR 0030](../../../docs/adr/0030-p-29-policy-mediator.md).

8. **Trajectory write + Patrol monitoring.** [P-05 (ADR 0012)](../../../docs/adr/0012-p-05-trajectory-capture.md) appends the cycle event with FC handles in the payload. [P-06 Patrol-tier (ADR 0013)](../../../docs/adr/0013-p-06-watchdog-tiers.md) monitors the FC log for collusion / correlation patterns at coarser cadence (the P-34 patrol-tier distributional sweep is part of the same auditor pipeline).

**Survival-window state machine (load-bearing).** Per [ADR 0064](../../../docs/adr/0064-p-30-variant-d7-survival-window.md), the workflow type `SurvivalWindowWorkflow` (Temporal Python SDK) carries the authoritative state and FC commitment ID. Transitions: `FC-declared` (workflow start) → `opposing-side-running` (on `@signal opposing_side_started`) → `verdict-rendered` (on `@signal verdict_rendered(outcome, survival_window, counter_evidence)`) → `survival-window-open` (timer armed; if `survived` unconditional, the window may be unbounded; if `conditionally-survived-with-window`, the timer is set to expire at `survival_window.expires_at_ns`) → `window-expired` (timer fires; cascade emission begins) → `re-falsification-required` (all dependent FCs are signalled with `@signal parent_window_expired(parent_fc_id)`; downstream compounding-gate checks then deny until fresh refutation yields a new `survived` verdict).

**Work-unit definition.** A *commitment-typed artifact* — the artifact alone is not a work unit; the artifact + at least one declared FC is. An artifact with zero FCs cannot compound (substrate-enforced refusal per [d7-u-1.md §1](../substrate-requirements/d7-u-1.md)). Both Atelier-style issue work units and Refinery-style change-request work units are representable; the FC envelope's `artifact-kind` field is the typing axis.

**Distinctive methodology decisions.** Four:

- **Independence is measured, not declared.** Every survived FC passes the [ADR 0061](../../../docs/adr/0061-p-34-independence-auditor.md) three-layer audit before the compounding gate admits it downstream. F27/F46/F48 are closed at substrate, not at methodology.

- **Survival is timer-driven, not perpetual.** Per [ADR 0064](../../../docs/adr/0064-p-30-variant-d7-survival-window.md), even `survived` verdicts can carry a `survival-window` (unbounded for some `artifact-kind`s, bounded for others). When the window expires, downstream artifacts are flagged for re-falsification — F8 (stale-knowledge) is structurally addressed.

- **F53 (voluntary-discipline fragility) is structural, not promised.** The compounding gate refuses to expose un-survived artifacts; there is no "operator should remember to ask for a judge" surface to fail at. Per [u-1.md §6](../substrate-requirements/d7-u-1.md): "F53 is addressed structurally by the compounding gate."

- **F42 is honestly not closed at substrate.** Per [u-1.md §6](../substrate-requirements/d7-u-1.md): "F42 is honestly *not* closed at substrate layer (methodology-layer concern)." The operator-as-opposing-side cold-start regime and the Option-C human-of-record reviewer queue are methodology-layer attention-design items that re-introduce F42-style cognitive-ceiling surfaces.

## §4 Discipline binding

D7-U-1 binds 9 of the 10 discipline ADRs (0018-0027); honesty is bound but with a candidate-specific carve-out (F42 acknowledgement). Per-discipline binding:

- **Bias guard ([ADR 0018](../../../docs/adr/0018-discipline-bias-guard.md)).** Bound **structurally** at [P-33 (ADR 0060)](../../../docs/adr/0060-p-33-opposing-side-router.md): the three-layer denylist makes builder-family exclusion unconditional at every FC dispatch. F46 (single-model review blindspot) is closed by construction.

- **Cognitive escrow ([ADR 0019](../../../docs/adr/0019-discipline-cognitive-escrow.md)).** Bound at the [P-05 (ADR 0012)](../../../docs/adr/0012-p-05-trajectory-capture.md) trajectory + Patrol-tier ([P-06, ADR 0013](../../../docs/adr/0013-p-06-watchdog-tiers.md)) layer. **Carve-out**: D7-U-1's blind-axis test explicitly prohibits *interval-as-substrate / escrow-as-substrate primitive promotion* (per [bias-guards d7-u-1.md prohibitions](../bias-guards/phase-3/d7-blind-axis/d7-u-1-prohibit-interval-escrow.md)). The cognitive escrow *phenomenon* is acknowledged at methodology layer (attention-surface design for the Option-C reviewer queue); the substrate-primitive promotion is what is prohibited.

- **Cost ceiling ([ADR 0020](../../../docs/adr/0020-discipline-cost-ceiling.md)).** Bound at [P-02 (ADR 0011)](../../../docs/adr/0011-p-02-cost-ceilings.md) with FC-parameterised budgets: each FC carries `refutation-attempt.budget.{tokens, time, sample-size}`. D-5 ("Cost ceiling is the FC's `refutation-attempt.budget` field") accepted-with-justification per [d7-u-1.md §4](../bias-guards/phase-3/d7-blind-axis/d7-u-1-prohibit-interval-escrow.md#4-4-defaults-accepted-vs-challenged).

- **Holdout ([ADR 0021](../../../docs/adr/0021-discipline-holdout.md)).** Bound at [P-08 scenario storage (ADR 0015)](../../../docs/adr/0015-p-08-scenario-storage-with-runner-contract.md) but *generalised*: the [ADR 0063](../../../docs/adr/0063-p-29-variant-d7-fc-survival.md) compounding gate is the substrate-enforced holdout boundary at **every** artifact boundary, not only at acceptance time. Per [d7-u-1.md §4](../bias-guards/phase-3/d7-blind-axis/d7-u-1-prohibit-interval-escrow.md#4-4-defaults-accepted-vs-challenged): "This architecture *generalises* D-4 to every artifact boundary." F28 (holdout leakage) is addressed by the compounding-gate refusal at every boundary.

- **Honesty ([ADR 0022](../../../docs/adr/0022-discipline-honesty.md)).** Bound but with a candidate-specific carve-out: **F42 (cognitive-escrow negligence) is honestly *not* closed at the substrate layer** per [d7-u-1.md §2 F-mode coverage](../bias-guards/phase-3/d7-blind-axis/d7-u-1-prohibit-interval-escrow.md#2-how-this-addresses-each-load-bearing-concern). The operator-as-opposing-side cold-start regime + Option-C human-of-record queue are methodology-layer surfaces; if voluntary, they fail under F53. Carried as Phase-8 lean-eval candidate (§6).

- **Knowledge promotion ([ADR 0023](../../../docs/adr/0023-discipline-knowledge-promotion.md)).** Bound at the FC graph: a repeatedly-survived FC pattern (e.g., a deterministic-checker FC that passes across many code-change cycles) is candidate for promotion to a default-FC-template per `artifact-kind`. Promotion is itself a `kind: methodology-delta` FC (always cross-family-judged) per [ADR 0062](../../../docs/adr/0062-p-28-variant-d7-fc-envelope.md).

- **Regime classification ([ADR 0024](../../../docs/adr/0024-discipline-regime-classification.md)).** Bound at [P-33 (ADR 0060)](../../../docs/adr/0060-p-33-opposing-side-router.md): the `(artifact-kind × refutation-attempt.method)` dispatch IS the regime classification surface. D7-U-1 does **not** claim ADR 0028 P-19 framework — the FC envelope's `refutation-attempt.method` field plays the role classifier feature engineering plays in U-A/U-C, but it is **declared by the agent at FC-creation time**, not computed by a classifier.

- **Scoping ([ADR 0025](../../../docs/adr/0025-discipline-scoping.md)).** Bound at the compounding gate ([ADR 0063](../../../docs/adr/0063-p-29-variant-d7-fc-survival.md)): work-unit scope is bounded by the dispatched FC graph. No mandate-specific scoping.

- **Three-loop ([ADR 0026](../../../docs/adr/0026-discipline-three-loop.md)).** Bound at the Popperian conjecture-and-refutation loop (plan → conjecture → refutation → verdict → compound). The "compound" step is materialised as Patrol-tier monitoring of the FC log distribution + the [ADR 0061](../../../docs/adr/0061-p-34-independence-auditor.md) auditor's distributional sweep — meta-loop closure is substrate-enforced.

- **Trifecta closure ([ADR 0027](../../../docs/adr/0027-discipline-trifecta-closure.md)).** Bound at the [P-08 (ADR 0015)](../../../docs/adr/0015-p-08-scenario-storage-with-runner-contract.md) holdout + the compounding gate. Both substrate-enforced.

**Disciplines D7-U-1 is NOT silent on but does not own.** None — D7-U-1 carries all 10 disciplines. The carve-out on honesty (F42 acknowledgement) is a carry to Phase 8 + a methodology-layer concern, not a rejection of the discipline.

## §5 Mandate fit

D7-U-1's mandate-fit YAML block (in this spec's frontmatter) restated per work-unit-class:

- **initial-spec: both.** D7-U-1's substrate is mandate-symmetric: the initial-spec artifact is `artifact-kind = spec` with FCs declared per [d7-u-1.md §5 "Cold-start"](../bias-guards/phase-3/d7-blind-axis/d7-u-1-prohibit-interval-escrow.md#5-cold-start) (day-0 FC defaults are strictest: minimum two opposing sides per artifact-kind; operator-as-opposing-side mandatory on intent / architecture-sketch FCs; survival-window defaults to single-cycle). Greenfield: operator + deterministic checker as the day-0 opposing sides. Brownfield: legacy spec artifacts re-classified as wrapper-FCs become opposing sides on the initial spec. Supporting substrate evidence: [ADR 0060 P-33](../../../docs/adr/0060-p-33-opposing-side-router.md), [ADR 0062 FC envelope](../../../docs/adr/0062-p-28-variant-d7-fc-envelope.md), [ADR 0063 FC-survival DSL](../../../docs/adr/0063-p-29-variant-d7-fc-survival.md). **Falsifying scenario:** if greenfield initial-spec work units cannot survive their day-0 FCs at any non-trivial rate, the operator-as-opposing-side bootstrap is wrong.

- **refactor: both.** Refactoring is naturally FC-typed — the proposed change carries FCs against the artifact's `conjecture` (e.g., "behaviour-preserving against the live test suite"). Greenfield refactor (against intent-invariants): operator-attested + deterministic-checker FCs. Brownfield refactor (against live-test anchors + Brier pace-layers): legacy test suite is the rich initial opposing-side catalog. Supporting substrate evidence: ADR 0062 FC envelope, ADR 0063 compounding gate, ADR 0064 survival-window. **Falsifying scenario:** if refactor work units routinely fail the [ADR 0061](../../../docs/adr/0061-p-34-independence-auditor.md) auditor's trajectory-overlap check (because operator + LLM-judge share context), the substrate-level independence claim is wrong.

- **mvp: both.** MVP authoring operates against the operator-authored intent block — the day-0 frozen conjecture (FC of `artifact-kind = spec`). FC catalog thickens as MVP cycles deposit `artifact-kind = eval` and `artifact-kind = code-change` FCs. Brownfield MVP work is supported because the legacy artifact catalog gives the day-0 opposing-side density; greenfield MVP work is supported by the strictest-defaults cold-start regime. **Falsifying scenario:** if MVP work units never accumulate enough survived FCs to leave the operator-as-opposing-side cold-start regime, D7-U-1's earned-replaceability claim per [d7-u-1.md §5](../bias-guards/phase-3/d7-blind-axis/d7-u-1-prohibit-interval-escrow.md#5-cold-start) is wrong.

- **post-mvp-evolution: both.** Post-MVP cycles operate against a thickened FC graph on either mandate. Greenfield: accumulated `survived` FCs become the default opposing-side templates. Brownfield: legacy + accumulated. The substrate is uniform across mandates — the only difference is the FC catalog density at deployment time. Supporting substrate evidence: all five D7-U-1-specific ADRs. **Falsifying scenario:** if post-MVP cycles on brownfield require fundamentally different FC-template defaults than on greenfield, the unified-attempt claim collapses.

- **regression-fix: both.** Regression fixes are by construction close-to-anchor (the failing test IS the opposing side; the fix's FC `refutation-attempt.method == live-test` against that test). Substrate evidence: ADR 0063 compounding gate (the survived-FC is the failing-test-now-passing) + ADR 0021 holdout discipline. **Falsifying scenario:** if regression-fix cycles routinely route to long-survival-window FCs (rather than the natural short-window failing-test FCs), the substrate's FC-method-dispatch is mis-calibrated.

**DEC-1.a falsifier-discipline observation.** D7-U-1 explicitly claims `both` on all 5 work-unit-classes. Per [DEC-1.a](../decisions-captured.md#d1--unification-verdict-no-methodology-serves-both-mandates-working-hypothesis-falsifiable-by-phase-8), this is the strongest possible evidence against the no-methodology-serves-both-mandates working hypothesis. The Phase-8 lean-eval will pressure-test every `both` claim (per §6); if D7-U-1's `both` claims survive Phase-8, the unification-verdict working hypothesis is itself falsified for this architecture's domain.
