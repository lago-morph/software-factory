# Phase-2 contaminated tracks (historical record)

**Status:** Superseded. Preserved for provenance.

These are the original 9 Phase-2 tracks dispatched on 2026-05-24, against source files that contained contamination flagged by the Phase-2 axis-divergence audit and anchor-detector. Specifically:

- `failure-modes-v3.md` F57 was integrated with wording *"the factory classifies work units into automation-eligible vs human-required by stakes / risk tier"* — presupposing tier-classification as the factory's mechanism. The Phase-2 axis-divergence audit identified this as the smoking-gun contamination source for the unified-A + unified-C tier-axis convergence.
- `contradictions.md` Phase-1 bias-guard sharpenings (WEAK-1 through WEAK-5) were absorbed as if they were corpus-level claims; five Phase-2 tracks cite WEAK-5 by ID, treating a critic's framing as a corpus reference.

Per [decisions-captured](../decisions-captured.md) D5 (bias-guard-integration discipline) + D6 (full re-run decision), these original tracks were superseded by a fresh dispatch against cleaned source files. The live `architectures/v3/tracks/` directory holds the re-run outputs.

**Do not cite these files for Phase-3 merge.** They remain as the historical record of what the original dispatch produced and as evidence that the cleanup process was warranted.
