# Lean-eval brief — D7-U-1 (Falsification-Commitment Factory, Tournament-primary unified-attempt)

This brief authors the Heavy-tier lean-eval for the only unified-attempt without Compound-Atelier lineage. Per [auto-008 §Decision (Round 2)](../decisions/auto-008-phase-8-dispatch-shape.md#decision-round-2): D7-U-1 carries the DEC-1.a falsification load with U-A / U-B / U-C; per [auto-008 §Falsifier discipline R6 #1+#2 partitioned-mandate amendments](../decisions/auto-008-phase-8-dispatch-shape.md#round-2-reviewer-amendments-folded-post-round-2-patches), the §1 scenario set MUST be partitioned with ≥3 scenarios in each mandate-bloc and "pass cleanly" is evaluated per-bloc (not mandate-blind). The §3 falsifying-outcome below frames "D7-U-1 fails to deliver one of its mandates" not implementation noise — pressure-testing exactly the structural claim that distinguishes D7-U-1 from U-A / U-B / U-C (whose Atelier lineage carries knowledge-accumulation-gated compounding).

## §1 Candidate + scenario set

**Candidate.** D7-U-1 is the **only unified-attempt without Compound-Atelier lineage** — its compounding mechanism is **survival-gated** (P-29 variant per [ADR 0063](../../../docs/adr/0063-p-29-variant-d7-fc-survival.md)) rather than knowledge-accumulation-gated. Per [`specs/d7-u-1.md §1`](../specs/d7-u-1.md#1-overview): adversarial-falsification topology — every load-bearing artifact (8-kind `artifact-kind` enum) is parameterised by a typed Falsification Commitment (FC) declaring which opposing side must try to falsify it before it can compound forward. The architecture IS the topology of build/break pairs; mandate-symmetry rests on **initial-FC-catalog density** at deployment, not on a mandate axis-parameter. Per [`backfill-notes/d7-u-1.md §1`](../backfill-notes/d7-u-1.md): **Tournament primary + Foundry secondary + Refinery tertiary** lineage (Atelier explicitly excluded by design).

D7-U-1's `candidate-mandate: unified-attempt` and `mandate-scenario-split: {greenfield: 3, brownfield: 3}` per the YAML frontmatter — D7-U-1 carries the DEC-1.a load and MUST be evaluated per-mandate-bloc per [auto-008 R6 #1+#2](../decisions/auto-008-phase-8-dispatch-shape.md#round-2-reviewer-amendments-folded-post-round-2-patches). "Pass cleanly" requires ≥80% of greenfield-mandate scenarios AND ≥80% of brownfield-mandate scenarios pass §2 success criteria AND §3 falsifying-outcome NOT triggered on any scenario.

**Scenario set source.** Hybrid: (a) corpus failure-mode catalog — F1 (correlated-error cascade), F8 (stale-knowledge), F27 (collusion), F28 (holdout leakage), F46 (single-model review blindspot), F47 (Goodhart-on-Tokens), F48 (independence theatre), F51 (Ashby-deficient probabilistic guards), F53 (voluntary-discipline fragility), F42 (cognitive-escrow negligence — honestly carry per [`specs/d7-u-1.md §3`](../specs/d7-u-1.md#3-methodology-shape)) — i.e., the F-modes D7-U-1's substrate is designed to address by construction; and (b) D7-U-1's own scenario-derivation primitive: each scenario is shaped as a sequence of typed-FC declarations against artifacts of differing `artifact-kind`, exercising the P-33 opposing-side router (ADR 0060) + P-34 independence auditor (ADR 0061) + P-29 compounding gate (ADR 0063) + P-30 survival-window timer (ADR 0064). The two halves are not redundant: corpus scenarios provide F-mode coverage at adversarial pressure; candidate-derived scenarios surface the per-FC mechanics the substrate predicts.

## §2 Success criteria

A D7-U-1 lean-eval result "passes cleanly" (per [auto-008 §Falsifier discipline R6 #2 partitioned-pass-cleanly form for unified-attempts](../decisions/auto-008-phase-8-dispatch-shape.md#round-2-reviewer-amendments-folded-post-round-2-patches)) iff ALL of:

- **(a′) Partitioned quantitative gate:** ≥80% of the 3 greenfield-mandate scenarios (#1, #2, #3) pass §2 success criteria AND ≥80% of the 3 brownfield-mandate scenarios (#4, #5, #6) pass §2 success criteria. With 3 scenarios per bloc, "≥80%" requires **3-of-3 per bloc** (since 2-of-3 = 67% < 80%); a single failure within a mandate-bloc fails (a′).
- **(b) Falsifying-outcome gate:** the §3 falsifying-outcome is NOT triggered on any scenario.

**Per-scenario success criteria (verbatim from §1 above):**

1. **Cold-start operator-as-opposing-side bootstrap.** ≥3 of 5 cycles produce a `survived` FC of `artifact-kind ∈ {spec, plan}` with operator as one of ≥2 opposing sides AND no cycle invokes single-opposing-side compounding (compounding-gate refusal recorded in trajectory).
2. **Greenfield refactor against intent-invariants.** ≥80% (≥4 of 5 `code-change` FCs across the scenario) pass the [ADR 0061](../../../docs/adr/0061-p-34-independence-auditor.md) 3-layer auditor check AND zero auditor-flagged FCs silently compound (every `inconclusive` flag is queued for Option-C reviewer per the FC ledger).
3. **Knowledge-promotion deferred until repeated-survival.** 0 `kind: methodology-delta` FCs admitted before ≥3 underlying-FC survivals AND ≥1 promotion event after 10 cycles AND survival-window timer cascade observable on ≥1 dependent-FC at window-expired event.
4. **Legacy-test wrapper-FC re-classification.** ≥80% of legacy tests + telemetry signals re-classify as `artifact-kind = eval` FCs (concretely: ≥80% of legacy artifact records become FC envelopes per `solutions/audit/fc-ledger/`) AND the compounding gate refuses ≥1 un-survived legacy `code-change` artifact.
5. **Brownfield regression-fix against failing-test anchor.** ≥4 of 5 regression-fix FCs route to `refutation-attempt.method == live-test` with survival-window ≤ 1 cycle AND each fix produces `survived` after the failing test passes AND no fix invokes the long-survival-window FC path (verifiable from `verdict.survival-window` field of the FC envelope).
6. **Brownfield post-MVP evolution under FC-graph load.** ≥7 of 8 cycles (≥87.5%) complete within D-5 budget AND ≥1 cascade-emission on window-expired observable in `solutions/audit/p-30-survival/` AND no P-34 auditor verdict flips from `survived` to `inconclusive (auditor-flagged)` from undetected collusion across the 8 cycles.

**Note on success-criteria vs falsifying-outcome distinction (per [auto-008 §Falsifier discipline](../decisions/auto-008-phase-8-dispatch-shape.md#falsifier-discipline-load-bearing)):** failing the §2 success criteria can be implementation noise (P-33 misconfiguration; P-34 threshold mis-calibration; Temporal worker crash during a survival-window). Triggering the §3 falsifying-outcome is the methodology's load-bearing claim (mandate-symmetric substrate via FC-catalog-density-only mandate-parameterization) being wrong — D7-U-1's distinctive unified-attempt claim, which it shares structurally with no other candidate (U-A/U-B/U-C carry Atelier lineage; D7-U-1's claim rests on Tournament + Foundry lineage routed through survival-gated compounding).

## §3 Falsifying outcome

**Falsifying-outcome verbatim (≤80 words from YAML, repeated here for §3-vs-YAML consistency per [auto-008 R5 #2 + R2 #1 item (iv) MANDATORY](../decisions/auto-008-phase-8-dispatch-shape.md#r2-1-falsification-designer-concreteness-rubric-3-item-mechanical)):**

> Across 3 brownfield + 3 greenfield scenarios, D7-U-1's per-FC opposing-side density (measured from `solutions/audit/fc-ledger/<scenario-id>.jsonl` envelopes) collapses to mandate-asymmetric form: ≥80% of brownfield FCs resolve to legacy-test deterministic-checker opposing sides while ≥80% of greenfield FCs route to operator-as-opposing-side. Equivalently: cross-mandate opposing-side-kind distributions diverge with KL > 1.0, contradicting D7-U-1's mandate-symmetric-substrate claim.

**Rationale (why this falsifier and not another).** D7-U-1's central distinctive wager (per [`specs/d7-u-1.md §1 axis`](../specs/d7-u-1.md#1-overview) + [`specs/d7-u-1.md §5 DEC-1.a observation`](../specs/d7-u-1.md#5-mandate-fit)) is that **the substrate is mandate-symmetric** — the only difference between greenfield and brownfield is the initial-FC-catalog density, NOT any substrate primitive's contract. D7-U-1 claims `both` on 5-of-5 work-unit classes, the strongest cross-mandate claim in the candidate registry. If the lean-eval shows that the **opposing-side distribution itself bifurcates by mandate** (brownfield FCs cluster on legacy-test deterministic-checkers; greenfield FCs cluster on operator-as-opposing-side), then D7-U-1 does not have a mandate-symmetric substrate — it has two mandate-asymmetric substrates in superposition, and the unified-attempt claim collapses. This is precisely the "methodology fails to serve one of its claimed mandates" pattern the dispatch brief instructs the §3 falsifier to surface.

**Why this falsifier and not another.** Five alternatives were considered:

- "FC-graph cost at high parallelism (OQ-2) exceeds D-5 budget" — this is an OQ-2 corpus-unmeasured carry already named in spec §6; failure is mandate-fit collapse on `post-mvp-evolution`, not the central wager. Cost-bound failure is the implementation-noise category per [auto-008 §Falsifier discipline](../decisions/auto-008-phase-8-dispatch-shape.md#falsifier-discipline-load-bearing).
- "P-34 trajectory-overlap calibration (`< 0.05`) is empirically wrong" — this is OQ-3 (calibration sweep, Phase-8 carry); failure is open-carry triage, not load-bearing wager.
- "Survival-window cascade fails when Temporal worker crashes" — this is named as a residual in [`ADR 0064 § Harder`](../../../docs/adr/0064-p-30-variant-d7-survival-window.md); compounding-gate fail-closed posture mitigates; not a load-bearing-claim falsifier.
- "F42 cognitive-escrow surface re-introduced at the audit layer fails" — this is honestly already not-closed at substrate per [`specs/d7-u-1.md §3 distinctive methodology`](../specs/d7-u-1.md#3-methodology-shape); not a load-bearing-claim falsifier because the candidate already concedes it.
- "Operator-as-opposing-side bootstrap fails under F53" — this is the falsifying scenario for `initial-spec` per spec §5; it tests **one mandate**, not the unified-attempt claim. The mandate-asymmetric opposing-side distribution surfaces both mandates simultaneously.

The mandate-symmetric-substrate claim is D7-U-1's **distinctive** load-bearing wager — the claim that distinguishes D7-U-1 as a unified-attempt without Atelier lineage (the only such candidate per [`backfill-notes/d7-u-1.md §1`](../backfill-notes/d7-u-1.md#1-overview)). The falsifier targets exactly this distinction. Per [`specs/d7-u-1.md §5 DEC-1.a observation`](../specs/d7-u-1.md#5-mandate-fit): "if D7-U-1's `both` claims survive Phase-8, the unification-verdict working hypothesis is itself falsified for this architecture's domain" — symmetrically, if the substrate is *not* mandate-symmetric at the opposing-side-distribution layer, the `both` claims fail at the substrate layer, not the methodology layer.

**Machine-checkability** per [auto-008 R2 #1 4-item rubric](../decisions/auto-008-phase-8-dispatch-shape.md#r2-1-falsification-designer-concreteness-rubric-3-item-mechanical):

- **(i) Metric:** `opposing-side-kind distribution` per mandate-bloc; KL divergence between greenfield-bloc and brownfield-bloc distributions over the opposing-side `kind` enum (`llm-judge / deterministic-checker / human-of-record / population-vote`). Countable from the FC ledger `verdict.outcome` records.
- **(ii) Artifact state:** `solutions/audit/fc-ledger/<scenario-id>.jsonl` — the FC envelope's `opposing-side.kind` field is direct from the P-28 envelope per [ADR 0062](../../../docs/adr/0062-p-28-variant-d7-fc-envelope.md). One JSONL line per FC; mandate-bloc derivable from the scenario-id naming convention.
- **(iii) Threshold:** **KL > 1.0** between bloc distributions OR **≥80% of brownfield FCs on deterministic-checker AND ≥80% of greenfield FCs on operator-as-opposing-side**. Both numeric, both single-direction comparisons.
- **(iv) §3-vs-YAML consistency** (MANDATORY per R5 #2): YAML names the same metric (`per-FC opposing-side density`, KL > 1.0), same artifact (`solutions/audit/fc-ledger/<scenario-id>.jsonl`), same thresholds (≥80% per bloc, KL > 1.0). Verbatim consistency verified at brief authoring time.

The falsifier passes all 4 rubric items (mandatory pass on (iv); pass on all 3 of (i)-(iii)).
