## §1 Primitive list (buildability-confirmed)

U-C requires five substrate primitives per [unified-C §1](../tracks/unified-C.md):

- **[P-28 — Typed-object store (content-addressed, append-only)](../primitives/P-28-typed-object-store.md).** U-C variant: the **AnchorObject** envelope (`kind` / `content` / `frozen-since` / `owning-mandate` / `mutation-protocol`). The durable anchor catalog the distance estimator reads against; the same store carries the `anchor-edit` write surface (§3). Verdict: `designed-system` (commodity substrate; envelope schema is the design content).
- **[P-32 — Distance estimator (multi-component, typed)](../primitives/P-32-distance-estimator.md).** Computes `distance(work-unit, anchor-set) → DistanceTuple{graph_distance, pace_layer_crossings, intent_field_touches, contradiction_flag}`. Composes P-22 + P-23 for the structural leg, a deterministic decision table for the pace-layer leg, and a P-14-routed LLM judge for the implicit semantic-touch leg. Verdict: `designed-system` for construction; **partial RG on calibration + Goodhart-resistance** (§2).
- **[P-19 — Eligibility / regime classifier (distance-gated dispatcher variant)](../primitives/P-19-eligibility-regime-classifier.md).** U-C names this the *distance-gated dispatcher*. Reads the P-32 DistanceTuple, applies thresholds `τ_low` / `τ_high`, emits regime ∈ {near-anchor lights-out, mid-distance Augmentation, far-anchor / anchor-edit human-required}. Verdict: `designed-system`.
- **[P-28 — anchor mutation queue (write surface)](../primitives/P-28-typed-object-store.md).** Not a separate primitive — a typed-filter view over P-28 (`kind=anchor-edit` envelopes) with named-human L4 approval gating against the target anchor's `mutation-protocol`. Covered by the AnchorObject envelope schema (§3).
- **[P-05 — Trajectory capture (distance-keyed variant)](../primitives/cluster-C2.md).** Per-event log additionally appends the P-32 DistanceTuple at write time so Patrol-tier P-06 monitors the empirical distance distribution for F47 gaming. Verdict: `commodity`.

P-06 is consumed (Patrol monitoring) but not owned by U-C; commodity per [cluster-C2 § P-06](../primitives/cluster-C2.md).

## §2 RG primitives

Per the [Phase-3.5.5 RG-primitive rule](../candidate-registry.md#phase-355-rule-on-load-bearing-rg-primitives-binding-user-approved-2026-05-25), U-C carries **partial RG flags on P-32 calibration + Goodhart-resistance** — not a full load-bearing RG primitive. U-C does NOT appear in the [Phase-3.5.5 application table](../candidate-registry.md#phase-355-rule-on-load-bearing-rg-primitives-binding-user-approved-2026-05-25) (which catalogues full RG primitives P-31 / P-26 conventional+invariant / P-34). U-C's partial-RG is on sub-components of an otherwise `designed-system` primitive, matching the shape of GF-S contradiction-detector, GF-C 2-field substance-check, and BF-M brief-quality calibration (per [registry close](../candidate-registry.md#phase-355-close)).

- **Calibration partial-RG.** Per [P-32 § Calibration uncertainty](../primitives/P-32-distance-estimator.md): no corpus recipe maps weights `(w_g, w_p, w_i)` and thresholds `(τ_low, τ_high)` to operator-meaningful risk. Plausible paths (historical-incident regression; operator-rated calibration sample) need empirical study.
- **Goodhart-resistance partial-RG.** Per [P-32 § Goodhart resistance](../primitives/P-32-distance-estimator.md): 2 of 3 legs structurally Goodhart-resistant (gaming `graph_distance` requires real symbol-reach cost; gaming `pace_layer_crossings` requires mis-typing the anchor, an `anchor-edit` always routed L4). The third leg, `intent_field_touches` LLM-judged, is **F33/F51-vulnerable** with a Patrol-tier residual detector only — does **not** close F47.

**Choice:** (b) accept-as-RG on both portions, routed to Phase 5/8. U-C does not commit to a bounded authoring sub-track. Phase-5 ADRs land `accepted-with-RG-flag` on the P-32 calibration recipe and the F33/F51 residual Patrol-detector spec; Phase-6 spec carries the accept-as-RG note in its YAML header; Phase-8 pressure-tests the degradation (calibration sweep + Goodhart-gaming red-team). The substrate exposes `(w_g, w_p, w_i, τ_low, τ_high)` as versioned parameters so Phase-8 sweeps are tractable — matching the GF-M / P-21 pattern.

## §3 Candidate-specific contracts on each primitive

U-C names two contested primitives (P-28, P-19) plus one designed-system primitive (P-32) and one commodity primitive (P-05, distance-keyed variant). Fixed-header contracts on the contested primitives follow.

**envelope schema:** (P-28 — U-C AnchorObject variant). Per [P-28 § U-C anchor envelope](../primitives/P-28-typed-object-store.md): the typed envelope carries `kind` ∈ {intent-invariant, architecture-rule, standards-rule, live-test, runtime-trace}, `content` (immutable bytes), `frozen-since` (timestamp), `owning-mandate` ∈ {greenfield, brownfield, both}, and `mutation-protocol` (named-human L4 policy reference). The typed filter is keyed on `kind` × `owning-mandate`; the P-32 distance estimator reads the anchor set produced by this filter. U-C's envelope is **immutability-metadata-first** — `frozen-since` and `mutation-protocol` are first-class fields, not policy attached out-of-band. The anchor mutation queue is a typed-filter view (`kind=anchor-edit` envelopes with `proposed-content` + `target-anchor-hash`); approval gates against the target anchor's `mutation-protocol` (whether enforced at a P-29 mediator or the P-19 dispatcher is a Phase-5 ADR).

**feature source:** (P-19 — U-C distance-gated dispatcher variant). Per [P-19 § U-C distance-gated dispatcher](../primitives/P-19-eligibility-regime-classifier.md): U-C's feature source is the **P-32 DistanceTuple** — not work-unit-class features (as in GF-S), not Codebase-Model-region features (as in BF-L). The dispatcher consumes `(graph_distance, pace_layer_crossings, intent_field_touches, contradiction_flag)` and emits regime ∈ {lights-out, cross-model-judging, human-required} via threshold logic on `w_g · graph_distance + w_p · pace_layer_crossings + w_i · intent_field_touches`, plus a hard floor: `contradiction_flag = true` ⇒ human-required regardless of composite (the F37 catch). U-C calls this *dispatcher* rather than *classifier* because P-32 already did the feature engineering.

**P-32 + P-05 (non-contested).** Per [P-32](../primitives/P-32-distance-estimator.md) and [cluster-C2 § P-05](../primitives/cluster-C2.md): the DistanceTuple is canonical (weight schemes are versioned config); `intent_field_touches` uses a stored P-13-reconciled `symbol → intent-field` map with P-14 judge fallback; P-05 appends the tuple at write time for Patrol distribution-drift monitoring.

## §4 X_UNM_B articulation

Per the [X_UNM_B finding](../candidate-registry.md#u-a--escrow-graph-factory-cycle--directed-graph-of-typed-nodes), U-C addresses Codebase-Model acquisition from legacy artifacts as follows:

- **Source artifact:** For brownfield, U-C needs the **dependency graph** (BF-L's P-26 *structural view*) for `graph_distance`, plus a **pace-layer mapping** (file globs → Brier pace-layer) for `pace_layer_crossings`. The anchor set is operator-authored at deployment time (production traces → `kind=runtime-trace`; tests → `kind=live-test`; ARCHITECTURE.md → `kind=architecture-rule` per [unified-C §1 primitive #1](../tracks/unified-C.md)).
- **Extraction primitive:** [P-22 polyglot codebase index](../primitives/P-22-polyglot-codebase-index.md) + [P-23 dependency-impact graph](../primitives/P-23-dependency-impact-graph.md), both `designed-system` (registry: High) — the structural view of BF-L's P-26. U-C depends only on the **structural view portion** of P-26; the 4-of-6 `designed-system` views suffice and the 2 RG views (conventional / invariant) are not required for distance computation. U-C therefore does **not inherit P-26's RG flag** (structural is non-RG per [P-26 § Buildability verdict](../primitives/P-26-codebase-model.md)). Pace-layer mapping is a deployment-authored decision table — `designed-system` on top of the structural view.
- **Completeness gap:** `intent_field_touches` is the load-bearing gap. The El-Kaim 9-field intent block is a greenfield artifact; legacy codebases do not carry intent fields explicitly. Both halves of the leg (explicit `intent-field:<id>` markers; implicit semantic touches via P-14-routed judge against an intent block) presuppose an intent block. For pure-brownfield deployment with no intent block authored, the leg requires operator-authored mapping from legacy spec artifacts (requirement docs, READMEs, tickets) to a synthesised intent block — the F33/F51-vulnerable leg from §2 *plus* an additional brownfield acquisition cost.
- **Fallback if missing:** If `intent_field_touches` cannot be computed, the leg **degrades to "operator-attested"** — the work-unit author declares a Boolean estimate per anchor, recorded as a typed P-05 event with role provenance. The dispatcher treats the leg as missing-and-attested and **falls back to L3 dispatch for that leg** (mid-distance Augmentation default); graph_distance + pace_layer_crossings continue to drive the composite deterministically; Patrol monitors the attestation distribution for drift. Graceful degradation honestly flagged in the trajectory — not silent failure.
