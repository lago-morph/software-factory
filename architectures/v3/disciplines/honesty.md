# Discipline: Honesty / no-fabrication

Substrate primitives carry construction paths or are flagged research-grade; corpus citations are required for the *why*; tracks do not pretend evidence they lack. *"It is handwaving to just assume something like `CodebaseModel` just exists"* ([phase-3.4-decisions-resolved.md](../phase-3.4-decisions-resolved.md)). The discipline operates at the architecture-authoring level (§6 *"What this track is NOT trying to be"*; §7 *"Open questions"*) and at the substrate-primitive level (buildability + corpus-why two-part rule).

## Open questions

- **No candidate disagrees.** The discipline is consensus across tracks (each §6 + §7 section follows the same shape).
- **The discipline's relation to RG-flagging at the substrate-primitive level.** Substrate primitives without a corpus-why are removed; substrate primitives with RG (research-grade) construction paths are kept but flagged. This is Subagent-2's surface, not strongly visible in track scope.
- **Is honesty an *architecture-level* discipline or a *process-level* one?** Flag for lead-agent. It governs how architecture artifacts are produced and how candidates are evaluated, but may be better placed in a process / dispatch skill than in an architecture-level discipline.
