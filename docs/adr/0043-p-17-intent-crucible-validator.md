# ADR 0043: GF-C P-17 Intent Crucible validator

- **Status**: Accepted
- **Date**: 2026-05-25
- **Deciders**: lead agent (Phase 5 Wave 5.3a subagent)

## Context

P-17 is a GF-C orphan primitive: only [GF-C](../../architectures/v3/tracks/greenfield-cold-start-first.md) claims it, as the day-0 substrate-resident intake that captures the operator's Intent before any code exists. Per the [P-17 buildability sketch](../../architectures/v3/primitives/P-17-intent-crucible-validator.md), the primitive is `designed-system` with `partial` research-grade uncertainty: the *structural* validator over the 9-field El-Kaim block (identity, statement, business outcomes, capability scope, policy references, invariants, non-goals, decision seeds, guardrails/feedback sources) is commodity Pydantic v2 work with cross-field rules and a [P-16](../../architectures/v3/primitives/P-16-ears-gtwr-linter.md) delegation for invariant/statement form-checks; the *substance check* on `business_outcomes` and `capability_scope` is F41/F50-class semantic judgment with no known deterministic construction. The structural surface is already covered by GF-C's contract-default; this ADR records the substrate decision for the substance-check surface.

The forcing failure modes are [F41 — Under-Defined-Intent Debt](../../architectures/v3/failure-modes-v3.md#f41--under-defined-intent-debt) (greenfield `critical`) and [F50 — Architecture/specification confusion in typed objects](../../architectures/v3/failure-modes-v3.md#f50--architecturespecification-confusion-in-typed-objects). Without a substance check, the Crucible passes form-correct-but-substance-thin Intents through to the Council, bench, and downstream cycle — the [GF-C pre-mortem 18-month thin-intent cascade](../../architectures/v3/bias-guards/phase-3/greenfield/pre-mortem.md). [GF-C §2](../../architectures/v3/substrate-requirements/gf-c.md#2-rg-primitives) carries two **partial-RG flags** on this surface (one per substance-checked field) and routes the residual closure burden to the methodology Council layer.

## Decision

**Build the P-17 substance-check surface as an LLM-judge ensemble dispatched through the [P-14 judge router (ADR 0016)](./0016-p-14-judge-router.md) under a dedicated `substance-check` judge role, with rubric-driven per-field evaluation and a `vacuous`-flag return path to the operator.** The Crucible's `schema-validate(blob)` surface returns deterministic structural violations first; only form-correct submissions reach the substance pass. The substance judge consumes the parsed `IntentBlock` plus a per-field rubric and emits a typed `SubstanceVerdict` (Pydantic envelope: `{field, verdict ∈ {substantive, vacuous, borderline}, citations: [rubric-clause], reasoning}`). The rubric encodes three load-bearing questions per field:

1. **Does the field name testable acceptance criteria?** (For `business_outcomes`: measurable + plausibly attributable. For `capability_scope`: bounded enough that `non-goals ∩ capability-scope = ∅` is meaningful.)
2. **Does it name a falsification surface?** (What observation would refute that the outcome was achieved, or that the scope was respected?)
3. **Does it name a cost ceiling?** (What operator-stated budget bounds the work before re-Council?)

The ensemble runs as `judge(shape='cross-family', judge_role='substance-check', ...)` per the [P-14 contract](./0016-p-14-judge-router.md#decision); a `vacuous` or majority-`borderline` ensemble verdict is surfaced to the operator as an authoring-feedback violation alongside the structural violations, and the submit-to-[P-18](../../architectures/v3/primitives/P-18-rsi-declaration-ledger.md) call is blocked until the operator either revises the field or invokes the methodology-Council escalation path. Two partial-RG flags from [GF-C §2](../../architectures/v3/substrate-requirements/gf-c.md#2-rg-primitives) carry forward into Phase-3.5.5: (i) **substance-check reliability** (does the ensemble produce stable verdicts on thin-vs-rich Intents?) and (ii) **LLM-judge ensemble agreement** (does cross-family judging converge or fracture on this rubric?). Both are flagged as [Phase-8 lean-eval candidates](../../architectures/v3/substrate-requirements/gf-c.md#5-open-carries).

## Alternatives considered

**B. Deterministic linter (regex / heuristic substance-detector).** Extend [P-16](../../architectures/v3/primitives/P-16-ears-gtwr-linter.md) with substance-check rules. *Why rejected:* the substance check is *semantic*, not *syntactic*. "Reduce on-call burden by 30%" and "make the on-call experience better" are form-equivalent under any deterministic rule library; only a semantic reader distinguishes the measurable claim from the vacuous one. The P-17 sketch is explicit: "no construction is known that deterministically distinguishes form-correct + substance-thin from form-correct + substance-rich intent." A deterministic linter on this surface would either false-pass thin Intents (the F41 default failure) or false-fail rich ones with novel phrasing (driving operators to template-match the linter). See [P-17 sketch RGU flag](../../architectures/v3/primitives/P-17-intent-crucible-validator.md#research-grade-uncertainty-flag).

**C. Operator-only review (no substrate-side substance check; route 100% to Council).** Let the methodology Council layer carry the full substance burden; the substrate ships only structural validation. *Why rejected:* does not scale. The Council pass is family-diverse-model interrogation with operator-in-the-loop — expensive enough that running it on every Intent submission (including drafts) is prohibitive, and the [GF-C §1.1](../../architectures/v3/tracks/greenfield-cold-start-first.md) day-0 cycle expects multiple draft-and-revise iterations on the Intent block before the Council pass. A substrate-resident substance pre-filter rejects obviously-vacuous drafts at authoring time so the operator iterates against fast feedback; only Intents that pass the substrate pre-filter consume Council depth. This preserves the methodology-Council layer for substantive interrogation rather than burning it on triage.

## Consequences

**Easier:** F41 mitigation acquires a substrate-enforced first line of defense rather than relying purely on methodology-Council discipline (the all-Council shape would be F53-fragile under operator-pressure). The 9-field schema's two semantically-judged slots have a contract surface the rest of GF-C can rely on; downstream stages (Council interrogation depth specification, [P-11 cold-start bench](./0010-p-01-sandbox-runtime.md) construction, [P-18](../../architectures/v3/primitives/P-18-rsi-declaration-ledger.md) ledger commits) consume `IntentBlock.substance_status` as part of the typed envelope. The judge-router substrate is reused — no new infrastructure beyond rubric + role registration.

**Harder:** the rubric authoring is a per-field design discipline; rubric drift across versions invalidates prior verdicts (rubric-version pointer is stored on the `SubstanceVerdict` envelope). Cross-family ensemble cost scales with the number of substance-checked fields × ensemble size; per-call budget enforced via [P-02 cost ceilings](./0011-p-02-cost-ceilings.md) and trajectory captured via [P-05](./0012-p-05-trajectory-capture.md).

**Explicitly NOT promising:** that the ensemble verdict is *correct*. The two partial-RG flags from [GF-C §2](../../architectures/v3/substrate-requirements/gf-c.md#2-rg-primitives) survive this decision — substrate flags, not buildability blockers. The methodology-Council layer remains the residual-burden carrier for substance-uncheckable fields per [GF-C §5 Phase-4 methodology-spec carry](../../architectures/v3/substrate-requirements/gf-c.md#5-open-carries). Reliability calibration is a Phase-8 lean-eval candidate.
