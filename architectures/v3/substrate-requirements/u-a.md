## §1 Primitive list (buildability-confirmed)

U-A requires 5 substrate primitives, all anchored to the typed-node-graph methodology. Per [primitives/index.md](../primitives/index.md#u-a), four (P-28, P-29, P-19, P-30) are *contested* — other unified candidates name variants — with same-vs-distinct deferred to Wave 4.2 per the [scoping principle](../phase-3.4-decisions-resolved.md#scoping-principle-immutable-overrides-any-conflicting-framing-in-the-integration-brief).

- **[P-28 — Typed-object store (content-addressed, append-only)](../primitives/P-28-typed-object-store.md).** Interval object store: every cycle node is a durable content-addressed `EscrowInterval` record; trajectory (D-7) is the inside-the-interval event stream. Verdict: `designed-system`.
- **[P-29 — Policy mediator (declarative gates)](../primitives/P-29-policy-mediator.md).** Reads the interval's `policies` block, fires gate / log / sandbox / approval-gate / reflection-trigger / judge-diversity rules in order, refuses to close unless every declared slot has a satisfying upstream record. Verdict: `designed-system`.
- **[P-19 — Eligibility / regime classifier](../primitives/P-19-eligibility-regime-classifier.md).** Decides `automation-eligibility` ∈ {lights-out, sample-audit, escalate, human-required} and `work-unit-class` per interval at open time; the decision is itself a logged typed event. Verdict: `designed-system`.
- **[P-14 — Judge router (multi-shape typed)](../primitives/P-14-judge-router.md).** Routes interval-fired judge calls per `policies.judge-diversity`. Cross-family mandatory at stakes ≥ "modifies state outside sandbox" (F46). Verdict: `designed-system`.
- **[P-30 — Re-entry / event registrar](../primitives/P-30-event-registrar.md).** Typed event-state-machine for `EscrowInterval{kind: re-entry}` nodes; fires deterministically once policy thresholds (watchdog escalation, cost-ceiling breach, severity-class trigger) are met. Verdict: `designed-system`.

## §3 Candidate-specific contracts on each primitive

Per the [auto-004 Round 2 fixed sub-section headers](../decisions/auto-004-phase-4-dispatch-shape.md#wave-41-brief-shape-revised-per-reviewer-1-amendments), U-A's contested-primitive references use the fixed schema below. P-14 is not in the contested-fixed-header set and is consumed at its default contract (interval's `policies.judge-diversity` field is the router's shape parameter; cross-family mandatory at high-stakes intervals).

**envelope schema:** U-A's [P-28 typed-object store](../primitives/P-28-typed-object-store.md) variant is the **typed-node-graph envelope** (`EscrowInterval` schema per [unified-A.md §1](../tracks/unified-A.md#1-architecture-sketch)). Fields: `id`, `kind` ∈ {bootstrap, refactor, spec-author, review, merge, deploy, re-entry, archaeology, methodology-delta, …}, `pace-layer` ∈ {code, plans, specs, architecture, standards}, `priors.{out-of-tree[], in-tree[]}`, `policies.{gate, log, sandbox, approval-gate, reflection-trigger, judge-diversity}`, `classifier.{work-unit-class, automation-eligibility}`, `artefacts.{inputs[], outputs[], trajectory}`. Typed-filter primary axis: `kind` × `pace-layer` × `classifier.work-unit-class`. Distinct from U-B's layer-typed and U-C's anchor envelopes; same-vs-distinct deferred to Wave 4.2.

**policy DSL:** U-A's [P-29 policy mediator](../primitives/P-29-policy-mediator.md) variant expresses each `EscrowInterval`'s `policies` block as **closure conditions** in OPA Rego (Cedar an alternate path). Rego rules consume the interval envelope, the upstream judge-router verdict (P-14), the sandbox-runtime attestation, and the reflection-trigger confirmation; the mediator refuses to close unless every declared slot has a satisfying record. The DSL design content is the **interval-policy schema** — what counts as a satisfying record per slot; how `judge-diversity: different-family` is verified against P-14's provider-family tag; how `approval-gate: required | sample | none` interacts with re-entry-registrar transitions. F28 (holdout leakage) mitigated per [unified-A §4 D-4](../tracks/unified-A.md#4-4-defaults-accepted-vs-challenged).

**feature source:** U-A's [P-19 classifier](../primitives/P-19-eligibility-regime-classifier.md) variant's input features are drawn from the **interval envelope's `kind`, `pace-layer`, and `priors` fields** at interval-open time, plus the substrate-current cross-family judge agreement on the interval-kind's recent history and the cost-ceiling state. Distinct from GF-S/S9 (work-unit-class features), BF-L (Codebase-Model-typed code-region features), and U-C (distance tuple). Output `automation-eligibility` is consumed by P-29 and P-30. Per [unified-A §5.4](../tracks/unified-A.md#54-day-0--day-n-trajectory), day-0 default is `escalate` for every interval-kind until declared threshold-bars are measured; enforced as an OPA-post-check substrate floor, not a methodology choice.

**state-machine semantics:** U-A's [P-30 re-entry registrar](../primitives/P-30-event-registrar.md) variant is the **re-entry interval state machine**: `in-flight → frozen → re-entry-open → operator-acknowledged → resumed | redirected | closed`. Transitions are driven by typed events from P-29 when watchdog escalation (P-06 Daemon/Triage/Patrol), cost-ceiling breach (D-5), or severity-class trigger promotes an in-flight interval to `escalate`. The frozen-state snapshot captures the in-flight graph position; the re-entry summary derives from the trajectory store + AILCCP immutable log. Subscribers: audit ledger, classifier, trajectory store. Differs from D7-U-1's survival-window registrar by being event-driven (operator decision) rather than timer-driven (window expiry); same-vs-distinct deferred to Wave 4.2.

## §4 X_UNM_B articulation

Per the [X_UNM_B finding](../candidate-registry.md#u-a--escrow-graph-factory), this candidate addresses Codebase-Model acquisition from legacy artifacts as follows:

- **Source artifact:** the legacy codebase plus its prior-cycle artifacts (history, tests, runtime telemetry, AILCCP immutable-log entries if present). Per [unified-A §1 "Brownfield ingestion"](../tracks/unified-A.md#1-architecture-sketch), the brownfield entry-point is an `EscrowInterval{kind: archaeology, priors.in-tree: [codebase, history, traces, tests]}` whose job is to produce the populated downstream interval-graph. U-A reads these in-tree priors as the analogue of [P-26 CodebaseModel](../primitives/P-26-codebase-model.md)'s 6 views.
- **Extraction primitive:** **no dedicated extraction primitive named in the U-A track.** The `kind: archaeology` interval is a *methodology-layer construct* — an interval-kind whose policies prescribe deep ingestion — but does not name a substrate primitive that performs the structural / semantic / dependency / test-coverage / runtime / convention extractions P-26 enumerates. The track relies on `policies.gate` and `judge-diversity: different-family` to enforce extraction quality, not on a typed Codebase-Model store. Honestly: extraction is methodology-layer-only in U-A as authored; the substrate sees only the typed envelope produced *by* the archaeology interval.
- **Completeness gap:** of [P-26's 6 views](../primitives/P-26-codebase-model.md), U-A as authored cannot reliably reconstruct the **conventional view** and the **invariant view** (the two BF-L itself carries as research-grade-uncertainty per [registry §BF-L](../candidate-registry.md#bf-l--brownfield-legacy-ingestion-first-1)) — U-A inherits whatever ceiling BF-L's authoring sub-track produces, with no candidate-specific mitigation. Additionally, U-A's typed-node-graph envelope lacks first-class code-region typing, so the **structural / semantic / dependency views** are reconstructable only at interval-grain, not at code-region-grain — coarser than BF-L's contract requires. Honestly: U-A's brownfield-fit claim per [§1](../tracks/unified-A.md#1-architecture-sketch) does not deeply articulate this X_UNM_B obligation.
- **Fallback if missing:** U-A's methodology defaults post-archaeology interval `classifier.automation-eligibility` to `escalate` (substrate floor; see §3) and requires `policies.judge-diversity: different-family` on every interval until the archaeology interval has been audited at a re-entry interval per [§5.4](../tracks/unified-A.md#54-day-0--day-n-trajectory). When extraction is incomplete, U-A degrades to operator-augmented mode (no lights-out) until the operator confirms the interval-class taxonomy. This is a *graceful-degradation pattern*, not a substrate-typed extraction repair; X_UNM_B articulation depth is honestly shallow and is flagged in §5 as a Phase 5/6 methodology-spec carry.
