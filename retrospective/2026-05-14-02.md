## Part 1 — what happened

### Phase 1 — Clarifying questions about repo structure

The user asked two structural questions:

1. What's the difference between reports in `/research` and reports in `/research/followup`? Will the followups be incorporated into the others, and will they influence `/architectures`?
2. When is `/research/00-synthesis.md` updated?

The questions had non-trivial answers grounded in `research/INDEX.md` and `research/PLAN.md`. I read those plus the comparison doc and synthesis files, then answered:

- `research/NN-*.md` are round reports (one per source cluster); `research/followup/NN-*.md` are thread reports (narrower deep-dives spawned by round-report questions).
- Followups generally stay as siblings; only Round-3 Thread 12 was folded back into a round report (`research/07-dark-factory.md`).
- Influence on `/architectures/` is indirect via synthesis. The architecture doc currently lags Round-1 only; `PLAN.md` §3.2 has the catch-up as an open task.
- `00-synthesis.md` is specifically the Round-1 synthesis. v2 was a re-anchoring pass once primary sources unlocked. It is not updated when later rounds or followups land — those get their own synthesis layer (currently `13-round-2-synthesis.md`; Rounds 3/4/5 + followups have no synthesis layer yet).

### Phase 2 — Strategic question: how does research become action?

The user then asked the real question: how will all this research condense into "usable action" — does the architecture doc get updated, or do the reports stay as individuals we work with one-by-one?

I gave a recommendation framed as a proposal the user can push back on:

- Three-layer funnel: **reports (provenance) → synthesis (condensation) → architectures (action)**.
- Closing the funnel means: (a) cut one unified synthesis that supersedes 00 + 13 + folds in followups; (b) revise architectures to v3 against it, probably collapsing the four specs to one chosen path + alternatives-considered; (c) promote binding choices into ADRs and a sequencing plan; (d) run the comparison-doc §6 lean evaluation before any orchestration build.
- Specific risk for the *lights-out greenfield* mandate: Atelier's strongest assets (queue, workpad, `docs/solutions/`) don't exist on day one. Cold-start design is a gap.
