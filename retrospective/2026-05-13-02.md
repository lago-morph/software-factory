- **`blocked-urls-round-N.md` per-issue files complement canonical `blocked-urls.md`** — the round files are detailed per-URL outcomes (audit-trail-grade); the canonical is the cross-round catalog. The two-file pattern stabilized across rounds 2 / 6 / 7.
