# agent instruction

**Adversarial-review amendment-inheritance from prior auto-NNN briefs.** When authoring a new `auto-NNN` decision brief that follows an immediately-prior `auto-NNN` brief as structural template (e.g., auto-007 inherits from auto-006), the new brief MUST pre-fold the prior brief's adversarial-review amendments at Round-1 authoring time — especially the audit-trail discipline amendments (commit-SHA pinning at both rounds, time-anchored honest-acknowledgement git commands, Appendix scaffolding for verbatim Round-1 reviewer-return text, 3-tier verdict menu commitment in reviewer prompts, TL;DR structure-not-conclusions, framework-ADR + per-variant pairing rule, PR-webhook-handling commitment). Failure to inherit means the new brief's regulator-angle reviewer surfaces ~9 defects already-solved in the prior brief.

*Grounded in: Phase-7 auto-007 Round 1 — Reviewer 3 / regulator surfaced 9 defects, 6 of which directly repeat auto-006 Reviewer 6 findings; Reviewer 3 explicitly noted "minor process bug, not blocker."*

# justification

auto-007 Round-1 Reviewer 3 (regulator/audit-trail) returned a 9-defect audit. Reviewer 3 itself flagged the meta-finding in its own summary: *"defects D1, D2, D3, D5, D8, D9 are direct repeats of auto-006 Reviewer 6 findings that should have been pre-folded as inherited precedent — minor process bug, not blocker."* The 6 inherited defects were: D1 SHA-pinning placeholder annotation; D2 3-tier verdict menu commitment; D3 TL;DR structure-not-conclusions; D5 Round-1 reviewer-return-text Appendix A scaffolding; D8 rewind-point SHA pinning; D9 time-anchored honest-acknowledgement git command.

Each of these was a Round-2 amendment in auto-006 (visible to anyone reading the brief). The auto-007 Round-1 author could have folded them at Round-1 authoring time by reading auto-006's `## Round-2 final amendments folded` section + Reviewer 6 sub-amendments. Instead, they were re-discovered through a full Round-1 adversarial review cycle.

Cost of not having the rule: every successor `auto-NNN` brief's Round-1 regulator pass re-surfaces the same audit-trail discipline amendments → wasted Round-1 amendment cycle + 9-defect regulator output that's mostly noise (D4, D10, D11, D13 passed; the 9 defects were mostly inherited). Estimated cost per successor brief: 30-45 minutes of authoring + amendment-folding work that's structurally unnecessary. Cost of adopting the rule: when authoring auto-NNN-Round-1, lead agent does a 5-minute review of auto-(NNN-1)'s `## Round-2 final amendments folded` + Appendix A subsections; pre-folds the canonical patterns. Estimated saving: 30-45 minutes per `auto-NNN` brief Round-1 cycle, plus the regulator reviewer's output becomes signal (genuinely new defects) rather than noise.
