## §2 RG primitives

Per the [Phase-3.5.5 RG-primitive rule](../candidate-registry.md#phase-355-rule-on-load-bearing-rg-primitives-binding-user-approved-2026-05-25), GF-C carries two **partial-RG flags** on P-17 Intent Crucible:

- **P-17 substance-check on `business_outcomes`.** The structural validator (form, type, required-list-cardinality) is bounded Pydantic v2 work; the *substance* check — whether outcomes are *measurable* and *plausibly attributable* — is research-grade per the [P-17 sketch](../primitives/P-17-intent-crucible-validator.md): no construction deterministically distinguishes form-correct + substance-thin from form-correct + substance-rich intent.
- **P-17 substance-check on `capability_scope`.** Same shape: the *boundedness-enough-that-non-goals-disjointness-is-meaningful* check is F41/F50-class semantic judgment, not deterministically decidable.

**Choice:** (b) accept-as-RG on the *substance-check sub-component*, routed to GF-C's **methodology layer** (Council interrogation depth). The substrate's structural-validator surface ships as bounded designed-system work today; substance gating is methodology Council work at Phase-4 methodology spec time, not substrate Phase-4.1 work. **No bounded sub-track is owed at Phase 4.1.** GF-C's pre-mortem 18-month "thin-intent → click-through-STIR → F40" cascade requires the methodology answer ([registry GF-C finding](../candidate-registry.md#gf-c--greenfield-cold-start-first-1)).

**Application-table row (per Phase-3.5.5 RG-primitive rule):** GF-C does NOT appear in the [Application to current candidates table](../candidate-registry.md#phase-355-rule-on-load-bearing-rg-primitives-binding-user-approved-2026-05-25). The rule was authored for load-bearing RG *primitives* (full-primitive RG); P-17's partial-RG portion is a sub-component (2 of 9 fields) and is routed to methodology, not load-bearing on the substrate. GF-C's framing: the partial RG *sharpens what the methodology must answer* (Council interrogation depth on substance-uncheckable fields), it does NOT move to a Wave-4.5 sub-track.
