# agent instruction

**Spec known-hard or unsolved gaps honestly under a scope bar.** When a component addresses a gap the source material flags as hard or unsolved, spec it honestly: partition what is tractable now from what is deferred, claim no more capability than is actually delivered, and register an unbuilt or partial item as exactly that. Never let a keep-it-minimal or is-it-addressed bar pressure a false 'addressed'.

*Grounded in: the v4 run -- C49 framed the unsolved counterfactual-replay (G19) as deterministic-now vs deferred-LLM, and the C57 register kept F54 objective-drift marked UNBUILT rather than over-claimed.*

# justification

A capability-for-principle bar that rewards "addressed" creates a quiet incentive to overstate coverage — to declare a hard problem solved because the spec gestures at it. That is the most dangerous kind of spec error because it hides the risk instead of recording it. This run's hardest components resisted the pressure: C49 (counterfactual replay, flagged in the corpus as "the most significant invention, largely unsolved") was specced as a partition — deterministic-tool-slice replay is tractable and claimed; full LLM-step counterfactual is deferred, best-effort, human-reviewed, with reproduction explicitly NOT claimed; and the C57 residual register kept F54 objective-drift marked UNBUILT rather than papering it as addressed. An adversary independently confirmed the C57 register's honesty (60 modes tallied, no false "addressed"). The marginal cost of honesty is a less impressive-looking coverage table; the cost of dishonesty is a self-modifying system whose real residual risks are invisible.
